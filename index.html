Every relationship carries a pattern that repeats — the same fear, the same distance, the same question asked in different words. Invite Me to Dinner exists to slow that pattern down, at a table, with someone trained to see it clearly.

About Andy

A relationship practitioner, not a lecture.

Andy works with the patterns people can't quite explain — the ones that repeat, regardless of who they're with. His approach is CBT-informed and hypnosis-informed, built to separate what actually happened from what the mind adds to it afterward.

  • Approach Cognitive-behavioural framework, adapted for real conversation — not clinical language.
  • Focus Attachment, trust, emotional regulation, interpretation, and boundaries.
  • Format One issue, one evening. No diagnosis, no ongoing programme unless you ask for one.
